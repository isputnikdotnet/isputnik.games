On a phone held upright, the screen sits on top with a pair of big buttons under each thumb. Turned sideways, it is the whole handheld. Tapping a corner of the screen also works.

Rules

  1. Four station modules launch sputniks down their rails. Robot РОБ-1 catches each one in his net.
  2. A missed sputnik crashes and a little alien climbs out and runs off. Three aliens loose and the game is over.
  3. While Belka the space dog watches from the porthole, a crash costs only half an alien.
  4. At 200 and 500 points every alien is rounded up again.
  5. Game B starts faster with more sputniks.
