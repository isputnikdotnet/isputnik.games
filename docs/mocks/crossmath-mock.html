How to play
  • Place every tile so each row and each column works out to its total.
  • × and ÷ come before + and −, as in school. Every step is a whole number.
  • Shaded numbers are given. Each tile is used once; two 5s are two tiles.
  • Tap a tile, then a square (or a square, then a tile). Tap a placed tile to send it back.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, digits place a tile, Backspace takes it back, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Rules fixed in review

  • × ÷ before + −, printed in How to play.

  • Each puzzle has exactly one solution (solver-checked).
  • Solved = every square filled, every line holds, every tile used.
